# Support Sapphire Star Codex

Sapphire Star Codex is independently developed and maintained. Optional contributions help support ongoing development, translations and game-data maintenance.

The app remains free, without ads or paywalls. Contributions do not unlock features, content or special benefits.

## Choose a payment method

Scan the code with Alipay or WeChat Pay. On the same phone, save the image and select it from your payment app’s scanner. Check the recipient and amount in that app before confirming.
