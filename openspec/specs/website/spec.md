# Website behavior

The global edition serves English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and
Japanese under `/sapphire-star-codex/` on GitHub Pages. The China edition serves
only Simplified Chinese at `/`, with an explicit link to the global website.

Both editions retain the real native feature screenshots, manual five-feature
gallery, keyboard/touch controls, local help search, light/dark mode, responsive
navigation, FAQ, About, support and privacy. Do not invent app capability or store
availability. Global Android is published on Google Play as `com.eurekaffeine.mhd`;
HarmonyOS has a verified AppGallery URL; its global download action uses the
original HarmonyOS NEXT badge. iOS remains pending until its listing is
verified. China downloads retain separate domestic Android availability.

Support is voluntary and grants no paid features or rewards. Payment occurs in
Alipay/WeChat, not on this website. The two editions use their respective original
Pocket Gallery QR JPEGs; keep dimensions, bytes and source hashes verifiable.

Preserve the established warm palette, system font and compact manual gallery.
Do not add tracking, automatic screenshot cycling or runtime external font assets.

Source and hosting branches are separate. A source change does not itself publish
the website. Global Pages serves gh-pages; China hosting requires an explicit origin.
