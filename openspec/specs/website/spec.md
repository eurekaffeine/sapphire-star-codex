# Website behavior

The global edition serves fourteen app languages, including Arabic RTL and
separate Spain/Latin American Spanish, under `/sapphire-star-codex/` on GitHub Pages. The China edition serves
only Simplified Chinese at `/`, with an explicit link to the global website.

Both editions retain the real native feature screenshots, responsive five-feature
story, keyboard/touch controls, local help search, light/dark mode, responsive
navigation, FAQ, About, support and privacy. Do not invent app capability or store
availability. HarmonyOS has a verified AppGallery URL; other platforms remain
pending until their listings are verified. China downloads use domestic Android
availability, not Google Play.

Support is voluntary and grants no paid features or rewards. Payment occurs in
Alipay/WeChat, not on this website. The two editions use their respective original
Pocket Gallery QR JPEGs; keep dimensions, bytes and source hashes verifiable.

Use Pocket Gallery’s centered landing scaffold, white light-mode canvas and
system typography, with the icon-derived rust accent (#B8533A). Keep the three
overlapping bezel-framed native phone screenshots in the hero. Desktop feature
chapters drive a sticky screenshot; mobile uses scroll-snap chapters and explicit
controls. Do not add timer-driven cycling, tracking or runtime external fonts.

Source and hosting branches are separate. A source change does not itself publish
the website. Global Pages serves gh-pages; China hosting requires an explicit origin.


The China landing and document footers display the owner-provided registration
link 苏公网安备32059002005040号 at
`https://beian.mps.gov.cn/#/query/webSearch?code=32059002005040`, alongside the
original Pocket Gallery public-security icon. The notice is keyboard accessible,
readable in both themes and wraps within narrow content widths.
