Features

Browse by game

Browse by game

Switch between three game databases. Search monsters, weapons, armor, quests and locations.

Monster weaknesses and hitzones

Compare elemental weaknesses and hitzones by body part. Check where materials drop.

Equipment and crafting

Check attack, affinity, sharpness, skills and crafting materials. Open related items directly from the equipment page.

Armor set search

Set required skills, fix equipment and limit results to owned decorations. Rise supports owned talismans and Qurious armor.

Saved entries and builds

Save monsters, equipment and quests for quick access. Keep your armor builds for later editing.
