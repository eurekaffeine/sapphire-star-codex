Frequently asked questions ​

Which games are supported? ​

Sapphire Star Codex covers Monster Hunter Wilds, Rise + Sunbreak, and World + Iceborne. Each game has its own data. Switch games from Home to browse the right monsters, equipment, quests and mechanics.

Can I download it yet? ​

The HarmonyOS app is available on Huawei AppGallery. App Store and Google Play releases are still being prepared. See the download section for current availability; store access may vary by region and device.

Does it work offline? ​

Core dex data is bundled with the app. Browsing, search, favorites and armor build tools work locally. Opening an external resource or downloading a future app update needs a connection.

What does the armor builder do? ​

Choose required skills and levels, fix equipment, exclude pieces, and search for compatible sets. You can limit the search to owned decorations. Rise also supports owned talismans and Qurious armor. Results depend on the selected game's data and your constraints; the builder is not a damage calculator.

Which languages are included? ​

English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Missing individual translations fall back to English. Website screenshots use the language selected on this site.

Is this an official Capcom app? ​

No. This is the project's own website for an independent, non-commercial fan companion. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CAPCOM. Monster Hunter, game artwork and game data belong to CAPCOM Co., Ltd.

How can I help? ​

See About & support for feedback guidance and attribution. Useful corrections include the game, entity name, current value and a reproducible source.
